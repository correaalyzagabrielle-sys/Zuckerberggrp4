Introduction to the Foundations of Digital Animation

Animation brings static images to life by displaying sequential artwork in rapid succession to simulate continuous movement. This creative discipline bridges the gap between technology and fine art. The core process relies on visual persistence, where the viewer's eye blends individual frames together seamlessly.
