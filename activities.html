Activity 1: Flipbook

Instructions: Create a flipbook that demonstrates the basic animation by producing the illusion of movement through a sequence of hand-drawn images.

Create a flipbook consisting of 40-50 pages.

  • Short Oslo Paper (1/8 size)
  • Pencil
  • Eraser
  • Markers or colored pencils (optional)
  • Binder clip on the left side

-Make only in between een each drawing to create smooth movement

-Keep the in appearance throughout the animation. Use clean and neat illustrations. Coloring is optional but should enhance the presentation if used. Secure all pages using a binder clip on the left side

-Write the following on the front cover: Name Grade, Strand & Section Title of Animation

-Test your flipbook before submission to ensure the animation plays smoothly.

-Be prepared to demonstrate your flipbook and briefly explain the animation process.

Pass the hard copy on the given deadline.

Performance Task: Mini Graphic Novel

Instructions: A mini graphic novel is a short illustrated story told through sequential panels, combining images and text to present a complete narrative.

Create a 6-page Mini Graphic Novel that tells an original story with a clear beginning, middle, and ending.

📌 Output Specifications

  • Minimum of 6 pages (excluding cover page, if applicable)
  • Page Size: A4 (8.27 × 11.69 inches) or Letter Size (8.5 × 11 inches)
  • Orientation: Portrait
  • Colored
  • Original title
  • Illustrations on every page
  • Dialogue and/or narration
  • Proper comic panel layout
  • Apply the Elements and Principles of Design
  • Use at least five (5) different camera angles
  • Submit in PDF or image format (JPG/PNG)
  • 📖 Plan your story before you start drawing.
  • ✏️ Keep your dialogue short and easy to understand.
  • 🎨 Use colors to show mood and emotion.
  • 📷 Vary your camera angles to make scenes more interesting.
  • 💬 Make speech bubbles neat and readable.
  • 📄 Avoid overcrowding each page with too many panels.
  • ✔️ Check your spelling, grammar, and page order before submitting
  • 💡 Be creative and make your story original.

Choose of the following themes or create your own (subject to teacher approval):

  • 🌱 Friendship
  • 🌟 Kindness
  • 💭 Dreams and Goals
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Family
  • 🏫 School Life
  • 🌍 Environmental Awareness
  • 💻 Technology in Everyday Life
  • 🦸 Everyday Hero
  • 🎉 A Memorable Experience
  • 🌈 Overcoming Challenges
  • 🤝 Teamwork
  • ❤️ Respect and Responsibility
  • 🐶 A Day with My Pet
  • ✈️ An Unexpected Adventure
  • 🎭 Fantasy or Science Fiction

Create an original work and enjoy bringing your story to life through illustrations.
